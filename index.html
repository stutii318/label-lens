The usual suspects

Common ingredients that regulators or studies have flagged, and the products they turn up in. Tap a card to decode a sample label.

Hormone + reef

Oxybenzone

Chemical sunscreens

Hormone-activity signals in lab studies. Harms coral, and banned in some places.

Hormone

Parabens

Budget creams and lotions

Preservatives with weak estrogen-like activity in lab studies. Human evidence is limited.

Hormone + planet

Triclosan

Antibacterial soaps

Restricted by regulators. Endocrine concerns and lingers in waterways.

Planet

Microbeads

Face scrubs

Tiny plastic that washes down the drain and never leaves.

Skin

Fragrance

Body mists and lotions

A hidden mix. A leading cause of contact allergy, and can include phthalates.

Planet

Silicone D5

Smoothing serums

Persists in the environment. Restricted in EU rinse-off products.

And the green flags

Gentle

Mineral sunscreen

Zinc oxide, titanium dioxide

Sits on skin, no hormone flags, reef-friendlier.

Gentle

Barrier cream

Ceramides, niacinamide, oat

Repairs and calms. Well tolerated by most skin.

Gentle

Mild cleanser

Coco-glucoside

Plant-derived cleanser that won't strip skin.

"Flagged" means a regulator or study raised a concern. It doesn't mean one bottle will harm you. Doses, skin type and your overall routine matter.
